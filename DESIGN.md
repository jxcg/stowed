# Stowed — Design

**Status: draft for the owner to edit.** Written 2026-09-19 from the decisions already taken
in `SPEC.md` (decisions 17 to 25), so the visual bag view (#11) has something to build on.
Nothing here is new; where this file and `SPEC.md` disagree, `SPEC.md` wins.

## Feel

A formal object you would keep, not a to-do app. Playing cards, luggage tags, passport
stamps. Quiet surfaces, one deep colour at a time, detail you notice on the second look.

## Colour

- One deep hue per surface, never two hues blended. Darker toward the edges.
- A second colour is allowed only on a frame or edge, and only as a tint of the same hue or
  gold foil. Never green with brown.
- Palettes are curated and named (oxblood, navy, forest, plum, slate, rust, teal, charcoal,
  ochre, bordeaux). Random picks always come from the set.
- System background elsewhere; the app chrome stays plain so the cards carry the colour.

## Type

- New York (system serif) for names, marks and anything on a card. Bold for titles.
- Small caps with wide tracking for footers and stamps.
- San Francisco (system default) for everything functional: lists, forms, buttons.

## Texture

- Film grain on coloured surfaces, heaviest in the middle, fading to the edges.
- One procedural texture per trip, drawn from its own initial, dates and name: a scatter,
  a monogram lattice, or a stamp ring. Faint, under the grain.
- A sheen and a shimmer band that follow the phone's tilt when the motion effect is on, with
  a holographic spectrum band. Off by default. Never under Reduce Motion.

## Marks

- The trip's initial and suit in two corners, the bottom one rotated.
- The initial again as a faint monogram in the centre.
- No emoji on cards. Emoji belong to bags and items, where they help you find things.

## Motion

- Tick animations bounce, with a haptic. Nothing loops, nothing moves on its own.
- Deck swipes are snappy; cards behind the top one shift, not the top one.

## Open

- The visual suitcase and bag view (#11). Direction: the same card language turned into a
  bag, one surface per bag, items as ordered marks rather than emoji stickers.
- Icon and launch screen.
- Dark mode is only checked for the trips page so far.
