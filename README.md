# Stowed

**Know what you packed. Find it when you need it. Bring it home.**

Stowed is an iPhone app for keeping track of the belongings you take on a trip, from
packing the first bag to checking the hotel room before you leave.

## Pack by bag

Create a trip and add your suitcase, backpack, or carry-on. Record items as you pack,
with quantities and notes for the details worth remembering. Adding something to a bag
means it's packed—there's no second checklist to tick off.

## Find things without unpacking everything

Search for an item to see which bag holds it. Browse a simple list or switch to a visual
bag filled with your items' emoji. Move things between bags as your trip changes.

## Check what's coming home

Start a return check using what you packed, then tick off what you've accounted for.
Mark used-up or deliberately left-behind items as not returning, and adjust return
quantities when fewer are coming home. Your original packed quantities stay intact.

An unticked item means you haven't checked it yet, not that it's missing.

## A little character for every trip

Browse your trips as tactile cards, with textured surfaces and optional tilt-reactive
lighting. Turn a card over for a quick look at what's packed, grouped by bag.

## With you, offline

Your trips and packing records are stored on your iPhone. No account or internet
connection is needed to use Stowed.
